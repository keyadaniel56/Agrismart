# **AgriSmart**
### Farm Intelligence & Management
*Empowering small-scale farmers with data-driven insights.*

---

# **The Challenge**

- **Data Fragmentation**: Vital farm data (yields, costs, dates) is often kept in physical notebooks or not at all.
- **Market Access**: Difficulty in finding fair prices and reliable buyers/sellers.
- **Financial Visibility**: Poor tracking of ROI and seasonal cash flow.
- **Connectivity**: Rural areas often have unreliable internet access.

---

# **The Solution**

AgriSmart is a **Local-First PWA** designed to give farmers total control over their operations, even in the heart of the field.

- **Offline Ready**: Works without internet via IndexedDB.
- **Comprehensive Tracking**: From seedling to sale.
- **Integrated Marketplace**: Direct farmer-to-buyer connections.

---

# **Key Features: Dashboard**

- **Real-time KPIs**: Instant view of Active Crops, Revenue, and Profit.
- **Financial Trends**: Comparative charts of revenue vs. expenses.
- **Actionable Alerts**: Notifications for harvests and order updates.

---

# **Key Features: Crop Management**

- **Lifecycle Tracking**: Monitor stages from *Seedling* to *Harvest*.
- **Yield Analytics**: Compare expected vs. actual yield to improve accuracy.
- **Input Tracking**: Detailed logs for seeds, fertilizers, and pesticides.

---

# **Key Features: Marketplace**

- **Direct Listings**: Post produce availability with location and pricing.
- **Smart Orders**: Seamless order placement and status tracking.
- **Transparency**: Clear history of sales and purchases.

---

# **Technical Architecture**

- **Frontend**: React + Vite (ESM).
- **Styling**: Vanilla CSS Modules (Modern & Lightweight).
- **Data Layer**: IndexedDB for local-first persistence.
- **PWA**: Service Workers for offline reliability and performance.
- **Auth & State**: React Context for robust state management.

---

# **Future Vision**

- **IoT Integration**: Soil moisture and weather sensor connectivity.
- **AI Insights**: Predictive yield modeling based on historical data.
- **Micro-Financing**: Data-backed credit scoring for farm loans.
